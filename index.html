About Us

Wiser Human is an AI safety organisation dedicated to keeping advanced AI under reliable human oversight. Founded in January 2025 by Francesca Gomez and Seb Ben M'Barek as part of the Catalyze Impact programme, Wiser Human draws on the founders' experience in technical and operational risk management to model threats and design mitigations to detect and prevent harm from advanced AI models.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make safe behaviour the rational choice for AI agents by designing the environments they operate within.

We draw on situational crime prevention and human compliance frameworks, test empirically across frontier models, and translate findings into controls that work in practice.
